-------

All content is fashion, beauty, styling, or video-prompt oriented and non-explicit.

RECOMMENDED COMBINATIONS
------------------------
Everyday fashion:
  1 garment + 1 color + 1 material + 1 hairstyle + 1 footwear + up to 2 small accessories.

Beauty portrait:
  1 hairstyle + 1 hair color + 1 makeup look + 1 nail look + 1 jewelry item.

Alternative/goth/cyber:
  1 outfit + 1 finish + 1 footwear + 1 neck or waist accessory. Keep hardware layers limited for cleaner generations.

Lingerie/swim/boudoir catalogue:
  1 garment/set + 1 color + 1 fabric + optional hosiery/robe/cover-up. Content is written for tasteful, non-explicit fashion presentation.

MiniMax H3 video:
  Start in 02_H3_Safe or use novoloko_v6_h3_safe_master.csv. Prefer one outfit, one stable hairstyle, one footwear choice, and no more than 1-2 securely attached accessories. Avoid stacking multiple dangling chains, veils, fringes, extreme asymmetry, or intricate loose hair.

H3 Director:
  Pick one quick combo or preset, then add only the identity/reference labels and timing required by the workflow. Preserve <Subject 1>, <Picture 1>, and similar labels exactly when used.

IMPORT NOTES
------------
- UTF-8 CSV with CRLF line endings.
- weight defaults to 1.0 and favorite defaults to False.
- index restarts at 1 in every individual file and master.
- category includes a top-level family and a clear subcategory separated by " | ".
- Individual folders are easier to browse; masters are convenient for one-node loading or randomization.

NOVA KREA CAMERA PACK V2 — README FIRST

CONTENTS
- 110 concise, stackable Nova/NovoLoko camera and framing snippets.
- 22 numbered category folders.
- Master, alphabetical master, and four best-of subsets in 99_masters_and_best_of.
- CSV schema: index,name,prompt,negative_prompt,category,weight,favorite

USAGE
1. Start with one composition or angle entry.
2. Add one face/framing control and one depth-of-field control.
3. Add a Stable Krea2 Combination last when you want an all-in-one safety layer.
4. Avoid stacking several contradictory angles or lens concepts.
5. Weight is a suggested stack weight; favorite marks especially reusable entries.
6. Index values are stable across category, master, alphabetical, and best-of files.

RECOMMENDED FULL-BODY FLOOR POSE STACK
- Full Body Floor Pose
- Overhead Floor Pose Clarity OR Floor-Level Reclining Pose
- Face Locked Floor Pose
- Deep Focus Full Figure OR Face-to-Feet Focus Plane
- Stable Floor-Pose Stack
Use only one viewpoint choice: overhead or floor-level. Leave extra margin around hands and feet.

RECOMMENDED LOW-ANGLE FACE-FOCUSED STACK
- Low-Angle Face Priority
- Low-Angle Face Lock
- 85mm Beauty OR 50mm Natural
- Moderate Fashion Separation
- Stable Low-Angle Face Stack
Keep the low angle subtle; the safety prompts suppress oversized chin, hidden eyes, and perspective stretch.

QUICK STABLE STACKS
- Editorial full body: Clean Editorial Full-Length + Head-to-Toe Safety Frame + 35mm Full-Body + Stable Full-Body Editorial.
- Beauty: Classic Beauty Close-Up + 85mm Beauty + Shallow Beauty Focus + Stable Beauty Portrait.
- Product/fabric: Full Outfit Material Study + 100mm Macro where appropriate + Stable Product-Fabric Study.

NOTES
- Snippets are intentionally concise so they can be combined in NovoLoko.
- negative_prompt values are safe defaults, not guarantees; remove any term that conflicts with your source image or intended crop.
- For close-ups, cropping feet is irrelevant; choose category-specific entries rather than stacking full-body controls.
